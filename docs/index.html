From code to considered review.

Your AI does the reading. The MCP supplies the standard. You make the call.

01

Connect the MCP

One command adds the OES server to your coding assistant. It runs on your machine and stays up to date.

02

Your AI diagnoses

It reads your code, searches the catalog, and cites the file, lines, and evidence for every match.

03

You decide

Open the offline report, mark Applies, Unsure, or Dismiss, and export Markdown or PDF. Recheck after fixes.

What your AI gets.

Your AI reads the code. The MCP gives it the standard and a review workflow, then turns its findings into your report.

get_oes_review_instructions

The review workflow, so every audit follows the same evidence rules.

get_oes_catalog_overview

All 15 categories and 804 practices, to plan coverage.

search_oes_catalog

Finds practices that match what the code actually does.

get_oes_practice

Full definitions, to verify each candidate before reporting it.

create_oes_review

Saves the offline report on your machine, so findings never leave it.

get_oes_practices

Batch definitions for every candidate in one call.

Backed by an open standard.

The Open Ethics Standard is a shared vocabulary for naming harmful practices. It isn’t a law, certification, or ethics score, and anyone can propose changes.

One source of truth

The MCP catalog is generated from a single Markdown standard.

Evidence with citations

Every finding points to a file, a line range, and an OES section.

Human judgment

AI can miss context, so you decide what applies.

Help shape the standard.

Suggest a practice, a correction, or a removal on GitHub.
