How to judge gray areas

Context matters

A real countdown for a real sale is honest. A timer that resets is not.

Effect over intent

A practice that predictably harms people is on the list whether or not anyone meant harm.

Legitimate uses exist

Collecting an address to deliver a package is not over-collection.

Many practices are legal

The OES describes ethical harm, not legal liability. It is not a law, a certification, or a checklist.
